01

Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to the PDF Infinity Android application (package name: com.pdfinfinity.app) and explains how information may be handled when you use its features.

This page is intended to be publicly accessible. It is not a substitute for the disclosures shown in the app's Google Play Data safety section; those disclosures must also accurately reflect the app and every SDK or service it uses.

02

Information that may be processed

The information involved depends on the features you use and the app version installed. It may include:

  • Documents and files: PDF files or other content you select, open, import, convert, or otherwise process using an app feature.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, app version, language, and technical diagnostics, where made available to the app or its service providers.
  • Usage and diagnostic information: feature interactions, crash reports, performance data, and related events, if analytics or diagnostics tools are enabled.
  • Advertising information: advertising identifiers, ad interaction data, approximate location signals, or other data processed by an advertising provider, depending on your device settings, consent choices, region, and the provider's configuration.

Important: The list above describes categories that may apply. The developer must remove any category that the app and its SDKs do not collect or process, and must add any category that is collected but is not listed here.

03

PDF files and document processing

PDF Infinity provides tools for viewing and working with PDF documents. When you choose a file or use a document feature, the app accesses the content and permissions needed to perform that action.

Do not assume that every operation is local or offline. The actual handling depends on the feature. If a feature sends a document to a remote server or third-party processor, the app must clearly explain that transfer before or when the feature is used, identify the recipient/service provider, explain the purpose, and state how long the file is retained.

PDF Infinity does not claim ownership of documents you select. You remain responsible for having the rights and permissions needed to use those documents.

04

Device permissions

The app may request Android permissions or use Android's system file picker to let you select documents and save files. Permission availability and names vary by Android version and app functionality.

Grant only the permissions you are comfortable with. You can review or change app permissions in your Android device settings. If the app uses any additional permission (for example, camera, microphone, contacts, or broad file access), this policy and the in-app explanation must be updated to describe its purpose.

05

Advertising and third-party SDKs

If advertising is enabled in your installed version, PDF Infinity may display ads provided by Google AdMob or another configured advertising partner. Those providers may process device identifiers, app interaction information, diagnostics, and other signals to deliver, measure, limit, and help prevent fraud in advertising, subject to their own terms and applicable settings or consent requirements.

Google's information about how it handles data in advertising products is available in the Google advertising technologies policy and Google Privacy Policy.

If the app does not contain ads, remove this section or clearly state that advertising is not used. The developer must verify the actual SDK configuration and consent flow before publishing.

06

When information may be shared

Information may be processed or shared only as needed for the purposes described in this policy, such as:

  • Service providers and SDK partners that provide app functionality, hosting, crash reporting, analytics, or advertising, if those services are integrated.
  • Legal and safety purposes where disclosure is required by applicable law, valid legal process, or to protect users, the public, or the service.

PDF Infinity does not sell personal information as that term is defined under applicable law. The developer must ensure this statement matches all actual data practices and applicable legal definitions.

07

Data retention and deletion

Information should be kept only for as long as needed for the purpose for which it was collected, to provide the requested feature, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations, or enforce agreements. Retention periods can differ between the app and third-party providers.

Files saved on your device can generally be managed or deleted using your device's file-management tools. For information held by a third-party provider, consult that provider's privacy and deletion controls. If PDF Infinity introduces accounts or stores user data on its own servers, provide a clear in-app and web-based account/data deletion process and explain how to request deletion here.

08

Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information handled by the app and its service providers. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

09

Children's privacy

PDF Infinity is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. The app's actual target audience, content rating, advertising configuration, and any child-directed features must be consistent with Google Play's Families policies and applicable children's privacy laws.

If the app targets children or includes children in its target age groups, additional requirements may apply to SDKs, advertising, consent, and data collection. Do not publish this wording as a substitute for complying with those requirements.

10

Your choices and controls

  • You can choose which documents to open or process.
  • You can manage Android permissions through your device settings.
  • You may be able to control or reset your device's advertising ID and ad personalization settings through Android settings, depending on your device and region.
  • Where required, consent choices for analytics or advertising can be reviewed or changed through the app's consent controls.

Your rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection, depending on the laws that apply to you. Contact us to make a privacy request.

11

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's features, data practices, service providers, or legal requirements change. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date. Please review it periodically.
